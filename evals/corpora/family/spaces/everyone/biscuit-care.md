# Biscuit Care Sheet

Biscuit is our beagle mix, born in 2021.

- Breakfast: one cup of kibble at 7:00 am. Dinner: one cup at 5:30 pm.
- Walks: morning and after dinner. He pulls toward squirrels, so use the harness, not the collar.
- Biscuit's vet is Dr. Halloran at Cedar Hollow Animal Clinic. Shots are due every April.
- He is not allowed on the beds. He may sleep in the crate in the laundry room.
- Treats live in the green tin above the fridge. Two a day at most.
