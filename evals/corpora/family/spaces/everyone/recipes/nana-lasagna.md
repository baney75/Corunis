# Nana's Lasagna

From Ruth Merriwether. Serves 10.

## Ingredients
- 1 pound ground beef and 1 pound mild Italian sausage
- 1 jar (24 ounces) marinara plus 1 can crushed tomatoes
- 15 ounces ricotta, 1 egg, a handful of chopped parsley
- 1 pound mozzarella, shredded, and 1 cup grated parmesan
- 12 lasagna noodles, boiled

## Steps
1. Brown the meat, drain it and add the sauces. Simmer 20 minutes.
2. Mix the ricotta, egg and parsley.
3. Layer sauce, noodles, ricotta, mozzarella three times. Top with sauce and parmesan.
4. Cover with foil and bake at 375 degrees for 50 minutes, then uncover for the last 10 minutes.
5. Let it rest 15 minutes before cutting.
