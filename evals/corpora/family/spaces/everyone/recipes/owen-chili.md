# Owen's Chili

Serves 12.

- 2 pounds ground beef, 1 onion, 1 green pepper
- 2 cans kidney beans, 1 can black beans
- 2 cans diced tomatoes, 1 small can tomato paste
- 3 tablespoons chili powder, 1 tablespoon cumin, salt

Brown the beef with the onion and pepper. Add everything else and simmer at least an hour. Mild enough for June; hot sauce on the side.
