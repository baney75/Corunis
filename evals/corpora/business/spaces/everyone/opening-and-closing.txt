Opening and closing checklist

Opening:
1. Disarm the alarm at the panel by the back door. The shop alarm is monitored by Ridgeline Security; if it trips by mistake, call them within five minutes.
2. Turn on the compressor and the lights.
3. Count the till to $200.
4. Roll the demo bikes out front.

Closing:
1. Bring the demo bikes in and lock the cable.
2. Count the till and drop the deposit in the safe.
3. Arm the alarm and lock both doors.
