# Shop Guide: Torque and Carbon

- Carbon seatposts: 5 Nm unless the post says otherwise. Always use carbon paste.
- Stem faceplate bolts: tighten in a cross pattern to the stem's printed value.
- Pedals: 35 Nm.
- Disc rotor bolts: 6 Nm with thread lock.
- Never clamp a carbon frame in the stand by the top tube; clamp the seatpost.
