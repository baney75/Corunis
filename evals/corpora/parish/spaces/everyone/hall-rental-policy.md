# Parish Hall Rental Policy

The parish hall seats 180 for banquets and 250 for lectures with chairs only. The kitchen has two ovens, a warming cabinet and a commercial dishwasher.

## Fees

The hall rental fee is $350 for registered parishioners and $600 for others, for up to six hours. A custodial fee is added to every rental; the custodian sets it each year.

A refundable security deposit of $200 is due when the date is booked.

## Rules

Events end by 11:00 pm. Decorations may not be taped to painted walls. Alcohol requires a certificate of insurance and a licensed bartender.

To book, call the parish office.
