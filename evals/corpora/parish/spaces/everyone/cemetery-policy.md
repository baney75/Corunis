# St. Columba Cemetery: Policies and Fees

St. Columba Cemetery is on County Road 9, one mile north of the church. The gates are open from dawn to dusk.

## Plots and fees

- A single plot in St. Columba Cemetery costs $1,450.
- A double depth plot costs $2,300.
- Cremation niches in the columbarium cost $1,100.
- Opening and closing is arranged through the funeral home.

## Decorations

Flowers and wreaths may be placed at any time. Glass containers, shepherd's hooks and solar lights are not permitted. Grounds staff remove faded flowers on the first Monday of each month.

## Monuments

Upright monuments are allowed in sections A through D. Section E allows flat markers only.
