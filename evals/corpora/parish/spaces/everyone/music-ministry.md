# Music Ministry

Music at St. Columba is led by four groups.

- Imogen Thrale directs the adult choir, which sings at the 10:00 am Sunday Mass from September through June.
- The contemporary ensemble plays at the Saturday 4:30 pm Mass.
- The children's choir sings once a month at the 10:00 am Mass and rehearses after religious education class.
- Cantors serve at every Mass. New cantors audition with the music office.

The adult choir rehearses on Wednesdays at 7:00 pm in the choir room.

All are welcome. No audition is needed for the adult choir.
