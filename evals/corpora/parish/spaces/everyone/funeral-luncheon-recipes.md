# Funeral Luncheon Recipes

## Ham and cheese sliders (serves 24)
Two dozen slider rolls, 2 pounds sliced ham, 1 pound Swiss cheese, a stick of melted butter with 1 tablespoon mustard and poppy seeds. Bake covered at 350 degrees for 20 minutes.

## Funeral potatoes (serves 20)
Two bags of frozen hash browns, 2 cans cream of chicken soup, 2 cups sour cream, 2 cups shredded cheddar, cornflake topping. Bake at 350 degrees for 45 minutes.

## Lemon bars
One sheet pan feeds 30. Use the recipe card in the kitchen drawer.
