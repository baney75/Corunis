Suggested offerings for sacraments and services (2026)

There is never a fee for a sacrament. These offerings help cover costs and are always optional.

Baptism: a suggested offering of $40, given at registration.
Wedding (parishioners): $300, which includes the hall for the rehearsal dinner if available.
Funeral Mass: offering to the parish at the family's discretion; musicians are paid $125 each through the funeral home.
Mass intention: $10.
