St. Columba Lenten Fish Fry

Every Friday in Lent, from February 20 through March 27, the Men's Club serves dinner in the parish hall from 4:30 pm to 7:00 pm.

Dinners include baked or fried cod, coleslaw, macaroni and cheese, rye bread and a dessert from the bake table.

PlatePrice
Adult plate$12
Child plate (ages 4 to 11)$6
Children 3 and underfree

Carry out is available at the side door. Proceeds support the parish school tuition assistance fund.

Volunteers are needed for the dish room. Sign up on the sheet in the narthex.
