Parish Council Minutes, October 13, 2026

Present: Fr. Anselm Marek, Teodora Vance (chair), Gregor Halvorsen, Philippa Renard, Martin Okafor, Hollis McBride.

1. Opening prayer and approval of September minutes.

2. Snow removal: three bids were received. The council chose Glenmoor Grounds at $6,800 for the season, which includes salting the walks before every weekend Mass.

3. Former convent: after the facilities study, the council voted to list the former convent at 44 Tanager Lane with an asking price of $389,000. Proceeds go to the building reserve. This is not to be announced until the pastor writes to the parish.

4. Christmas decorating: volunteers meet December 19.

Next meeting: November 10 at 7:00 pm in the conference room.
