# Facilities Study Summary (August 2026)

The study looked at the church, the parish center, the school and the former convent.

- Church: tuckpointing on the bell tower within five years.
- Parish center: elevator inspection current; replace hall carpet within three years.
- School: roof repair completed in July; boiler at end of life.
- Former convent: vacant since 2019; heating and wiring do not meet code. The council will decide its future in the fall.
